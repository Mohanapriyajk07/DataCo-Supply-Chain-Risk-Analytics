**DataCo Supply Chain Risk \& Performance Analytics**



📊 Project Overview



DataCo Supply Chain Risk \& Performance Analytics is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze supply chain performance, sales trends, product insights, customer behavior, and delivery risks using data-driven visualizations.



This project transforms raw supply chain data into meaningful insights that help understand business performance and identify potential delivery issues.



🎯 Objectives

\- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.

\- Identify late delivery trends and shipping risks.

\- Explore product-wise and category-wise performance.

\- Understand customer segments and market distribution.

\- Support data-driven business decision-making through interactive dashboards.



🛠️ Tools \& Technologies

\- Power BI

\- Microsoft Excel / CSV

\- Data Cleaning \& Data Analysis

\- Data Visualization

\- DAX



📁 Dataset

The project uses a supply chain dataset containing information about:



\- Orders and sales

\- Customers and customer segments

\- Products and categories

\- Markets and regions

\- Shipping modes

\- Delivery status

\- Order dates and shipping dates

\- Profit, sales, and quantity



📈 Dashboard Pages



1\. Project Overview

A landing page introducing the project, its objectives, and the dashboard's major analytical areas.



2\. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of business performance through:

\- Total Sales

\- Total Orders

\- Late Delivery

\- Profit

\- Monthly Sales Trend

\- Sales by Category

\- Sales by Region

\- Orders by Customer Segment



3\. Risk Analysis

Focuses on delivery performance and supply chain risks through:

\- Total Late Deliveries

\- Late Delivery Percentage

\- Average Shipping Days

\- Average Delay Days

\- Delivery Status Distribution

\- Late Delivery Risk by Shipping Mode

\- Late Delivery Risk by Category and Region



4\. Products Analysis

Analyzes product performance using:

\- Total Products

\- Total Categories

\- Total Quantity Sold

\- Average Product Price

\- Top 10 Products by Sales

\- Sales by Category

\- Quantity Sold by Category

\- Product Price vs Sales Analysis



5\. Customer \& Market Analytics

Explores customer and market performance through:

\- Sales by Market

\- Sales by Category

\- Customer Segment Distribution

\- Top 10 Customer Cities



💡 Key Insights

The dashboard helps users:

\- Monitor overall sales and profit performance.

\- Identify delivery delays and potential risk areas.

\- Compare product and category performance.

\- Analyze customer segments.

\- Explore sales distribution across markets and regions.

\- Understand shipping performance and operational trends.
